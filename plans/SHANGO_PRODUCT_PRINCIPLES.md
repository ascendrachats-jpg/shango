# SHANGO_PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES

Purpose
-------
This document defines the permanent philosophy that guides every future feature, architecture decision, UI improvement, AI integration, and product evolution for Shango.

It is written for engineers, designers, product managers, contributors, educators, and future collaborators. It explains what Shango stands for and the values that should never be compromised.

# 1. Mission

Shango exists to make world-class software creation accessible to every builder, regardless of geography, hardware, or financial resources.

Shango is designed for builders everywhere, with a special commitment to empowering students, creators, entrepreneurs, and developers across Africa.

Shango is not just a tool. It is a platform for learning, experimentation, and creation that brings professional-quality software-building capabilities to people who may have never had easy access to them before.

# 2. Core Philosophy

- Builders come first.
- Technology serves creativity.
- AI assists.
- Builders remain in control.
- Workspace is sacred.
- Every decision must respect user work.
- Determinism before intelligence.
- If something can be computed reliably, Shango computes it.
- AI is used only where creativity and reasoning are required.
- Shango enables builders, it does not replace them.

# 3. Product Principles

1. The builder should never lose work because of Shango.
2. Never destroy user work.
3. Never surprise the builder.
4. Explain before automating.
5. Fast for beginners.
6. Powerful for professionals.
7. Provider independent.
8. Workspace first.
9. Version everything.
10. Transparency over magic.
11. Architecture before shortcuts.
12. Quality over quantity.
13. Education over dependence.
14. Accessibility over complexity.
15. Respect every builder’s context and intent.

# 4. AI Philosophy

- AI is invisible: it helps without taking over.
- AI is replaceable: providers are interchangeable, and Shango manages the boundaries.
- Shango owns project intelligence.
- Providers generate content.
- Shango interprets, validates, and protects.
- The builder owns the final decision.
- AI suggestions are invitations, not commands.
- Shango chooses AI only when creative judgment or natural-language reasoning is needed.
- AI is a collaborator, not the owner of the workspace.

# 5. Builder Experience

Every interaction should feel:

- Simple.
- Calm.
- Fast.
- Predictable.
- Forgiving.
- Professional.
- Respectful.
- Empowering.
- Confident.

Shango should help builders move forward without confusion, erase friction without hiding control, and make it easy to recover, understand, and continue.

# 6. Long-Term Vision

Shango is a future where students learn software by doing, schools adopt a modern builder platform, professional teams ship products with confidence, and a global community cooperates around accessible tools.

Shango is a trusted software companion for creators and developers across continents.

It is a platform where learners become makers, where ideas become code, and where every builder can participate in the future of software without being limited by geography, access, or prior privilege.

# 7. Decision Filter

Before implementing any feature, ask:

- Does it help builders?
- Does it preserve workspace ownership?
- Does it increase trust?
- Does it simplify the experience?
- Does it respect architecture?
- If not, reconsider.

Use this checklist as a permanent guardrail for product choices.

# 8. Closing Statement

Shango is more than an AI coding tool.

It is a builder platform designed to help people create confidently, learn continuously, and own their work.

Shango exists to support the builder’s journey, sustain trust in the craft, and make accessible the power of software creation for every community.
